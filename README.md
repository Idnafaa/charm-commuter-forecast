A one-line weather forecast for Wobbleford, a city that does not exist — rain falls upwards till nine, then sunshine. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't” bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes one line (136 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The forecast

> Good morning, Wobbleford: rain falls upwards till nine, then sunshine — hold your umbrella upside down and your coffee the right way up.
